■   PROJECT / PORSCHE × DAVID GUETTA

Speed of Light

CLIENTPorsche × David Guetta
YEAR2023
ROLEFPV Cinematography
CATEGORYCommercial

Speed of Light is a commercial for Porsche together with David Guetta, released in 2023. André Larsen handled FPV cinematography on the film, bringing the camera close to the car in motion.

On automotive work an FPV drone can stay close to a moving car, follow its lines and pass through angles that a camera car or a standard camera drone cannot reach. That is the kind of shot André is hired for on car commercials and launches.

André Larsen is a Norwegian FPV drone pilot and cinematographer based in Dubai since 2020. He went professional in 2017, won the GoPro Million Dollar Challenge in 2022, and flies commercials, films and live events across the UAE and worldwide.

See the full list of projects on the works page, or read more about André and his background on the about page.
